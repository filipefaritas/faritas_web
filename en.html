Frequently asked questions

Before the first conversation.

Direct answers to the most important questions about the service.

Does Faritas provide legal or tax advice?

No. Faritas coordinates the project and organises the decision. Legal, tax, accounting, financial, real estate and immigration matters are handled by qualified professionals.

Can I engage Faritas for only one part of the process?

Yes. The scope may be limited to the Portugal Assessment, a specific stage or full coordination.

Do you only work with high-net-worth investors?

No. The main criteria are a clear project, compatible resources and a genuine need for coordination.

Do you support football players and sports professionals?

Yes. Sports & Executive Relocation may coordinate housing, vehicles, family setup, suppliers and ongoing support through suitable partners.

How are fees defined?

After the initial conversation, a proposal sets out the scope, deliverables, timeline and price. Any commissions or partner relationships should be disclosed transparently.
